Privacy Policy

Extension: Coursera Skip Video & Reading (Item ID: jjbgneddmjkolgmpamecbhfgjgdiajlf)

Last Updated: October 2026
Core Commitment: The Coursera Skip Video & Reading extension does NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data or browsing activity to remote developer servers. Everything runs entirely locally within your browser session.

1. Data Collection & Telemetry

We respect your privacy. This extension adheres to the following principles:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, usernames, passwords, or IP addresses.
  • No Tracking / Analytics: There are no analytics libraries, tracking pixels, or telemetry services embedded in the extension.
  • No Remote Developer Backend: The developer does not operate or maintain any remote server that receives user traffic or data from this extension.

2. Chrome Permissions & How They Are Used

To perform its automated study assistance features, the extension requests the following browser permissions:

  • storage: Used strictly to save your local UI preferences (such as English or Vietnamese interface language) and your optional personal AI API keys directly inside your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local).
  • activeTab & tabs: Used solely to verify if the active browser tab is an authorized Coursera learning URL when you interact with the extension popup or floating widget.
  • Host Permission: https://www.coursera.org/*: Used exclusively to interact directly with Coursera's official course progression endpoints on your behalf using your existing active browser session. Session tokens (e.g. CAUTH) are never exported or exfiltrated outside your browser.
  • Host Permissions: https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/*, https://api.openai.com/*, https://api.groq.com/*: Used only if you optionally provide your personal AI API key (Google Gemini, OpenAI, or Groq) to assist with quiz study. Prompts are transmitted directly and securely from your browser to the official provider endpoint. No keys or prompts ever pass through any third-party or developer server.

3. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your data with any third parties or advertisers. External communication only occurs directly between your client browser and the respective AI platform API if you choose to input your personal API key.

The popup includes an optional "Support" link that opens a donation page (donate.html, hosted on GitHub Pages) in a new browser tab. The extension itself sends no data when you click it, and no feature is locked behind donations. Any payment is handled entirely by the third-party donation provider (for example Ko-fi) on its own page, under its own privacy policy.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications in browser permissions or platform policies. Any revisions will be published immediately on this page with an updated revision date.
